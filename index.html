01 / THE INVISIBLE COMPLEXITYBEFORE ARRIVAL

A seamless stay begins
long before arrival.

A reservation begins with a promise: the room is available, the rate is clear, and the conditions mean what they say.

That promise travels through central property-management and reservation systems, hotel websites, global distribution systems, online travel agencies, and distribution partners. Each handoff introduces context that a simple price check can miss.

Rate integrity, availability alignment, cancellation terms, and bookability all shape the booking experience—and the decisions an operations team needs to make.

A difference is a signal to investigate,

05 / THE OPERATING METHODREAD THE PYTHON IMPLEMENTATION ↗

Make the difference
explainable.

01

Normalize

Match stay date, room type, and rate plan. Validate required fields, dates, finite non-negative rates, integer inventory, policy windows, occupancy, and booking flags. Reject duplicate keys.

02

Compare

Use decimal arithmetic for money. Check currency, occupancy, meals, tax basis, and promotion before comparing rates. Compare inventory, policy windows, and booking flags independently.

03

Investigate

Preserve the reference and observation. A missing baseline remains inconclusive. Ask for capture times, mapping, allotment, restrictions, and published conditions. This stage requires human review.

04

Route

Use the highest issue priority to suggest an owner and next action. Keep the case open until authorized operational evidence supports a decision.

07 / LIMITATIONS & NEXT VALIDATIONTHE BOUNDARY OF THE EVIDENCE

Confidence begins
with knowing the limits.

All data are synthetic. No internal hotel data were used. No OPERA, SynXis, OTA, or GDS integrations were accessed. This is a simplified operational exercise, and a difference is not a confirmed defect.

The fixture has one property, one-night rates, one rate plan, and no capture timestamps. It does not model multi-night pricing, currency conversion, effective dates, negotiated contracts, or production room mapping. Cancellation windows are compared numerically, not interpreted from policy text.

Before production use: validate property and occupancy keys, mapping rules, source timing, channel allotments, tax and promotion normalization, contractual conditions, and booking restrictions. Authorized system access and operational review are essential. No financial or guest-impact outcomes are claimed.
